The Loschmidt Paradox: Why Can't We Simply Reverse Velocity?

In classical Newtonian mechanics, each instantaneous collision is mathematically reversible (t \to -t). Yet if you invert all velocities in this chamber, notice what happens: after a brief, delicate re-clustering, thermal fluctuations and boundary collision rounding errors explode exponentially via the Lyapunov horizon. For macroscopic volumes (N \sim 10^{23}), reversing the arrow requires unphysical infinite numerical precision.

S : Thermodynamic Entropy

The macroscopic state function measuring lost mechanical work capacity and systemic thermal dispersal. In closed systems, dS ≥ 0.

k_B : Boltzmann's Constant

The fundamental dimensional bridge linking macroscopic thermal energy (temperature in Kelvins) with microscopic kinetic degrees of freedom.

Ω or W : Microstate Multiplicity

The exact count of distinct micro-configurations (positions and momenta) compatible with the observed single macrostate (P, V, T).
