## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a museum-grade, intellectually stimulating digital atelier designed for visual and interactive inquiry. It marries the typographic gravitas of prestigious scholarly journals and archival encyclopedias with the kinetic responsiveness of modern computational notebooks. 

Targeting researchers, inquisitive polymaths, students, and engineers, the interface inspires focused immersion and intellectual delight. The design philosophy merges **Refined Editorialism** with **Modern Tactile Minimalism**:
- **Warm Archival Grounding**: Clean bone, vellum, and warm slate tones replace sterile clinical whites, evoking high-grade paper and tactile curiosity.
- **Museum-Grade Hierarchy**: Expressive, literary serif headlines command attention and intellectual reverence, framed cleanly by precise, rationalist sans-serif metadata.
- **Chromatic Domain Attribution**: Scientific disciplines are brought to life through luminous, high-chroma spectral accents set against deep ink and muted neutral substrates.
- **Tangible Interaction**: Modules, interactive sliders, data nodes, and exploratory diagrams feature restrained tactile depth, microscopic framing outlines, and responsive feedback that feels physical, grounded, and deliberate.

## Colors
The palette balances an organic, archival canvas with deep ink-well typography and distinct departmental accents. 

### Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Default (`#F9F8F5`)**: A soft, parchment-tinted bone hue that prevents ocular fatigue during protracted reading.
- **Surface Level 1 / Elevated (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure crisp card containers and interactive viewports floating cleanly above the bone field.
- **Surface Inset / Recessed (`#F0EFEA`)**: Used for code blocks, diagram backdrops, and interactive workbench wells.
- **Surface Outline / Rule (`#E4E1D9`)**: Precise hairline borders framing interactive modules and separating editorial columns.

### Ink & Typography Hierarchy
- **Ink Primary (`#1B2228`)**: Deep obsidian-slate with cold undertones. Never pure `#000000`.
- **Ink Secondary (`#4A5560`)**: Subdued mineral slate for secondary commentary, captions, and section summaries.
- **Ink Muted / Tertiary (`#7C8793`)**: Neutral grey for micro-labels, keyboard shortcuts, and structural timestamps.

### Domain Discipline Accents
- **Mathematics & Physics (`#00A4B4` / `#0D828A`)**: Radiant electric cyan transitioning to deep cerulean for vector fields, formulas, and kinetic systems.
- **History & Archaeology (`#BD5338` / `#D97746`)**: Terracotta and rich ochre for chronology ribbons, geographic artifacts, and historical context.
- **Natural Sciences (`#1F8A65` / `#2DB885`)**: Emerald and field sage denoting biological, evolutionary, and ecological structures.
- **Systems & Engineering (`#5B48D9` / `#7B6BF2`)**: Deep violet and electric indigo for computational graphs, systemic models, and circuit paths.

## Typography
The typographic treatment generates a dialogue between humanist academic literature and modern computational analysis.

- **Headline & Display**: *Newsreader* brings optical elegance, featuring distinctive serifs with editorial cadence. Headlines should embrace natural italicization for Latin scientific terminology, mathematical principles, and key conceptual terms.
- **Body & Controls**: *Plus Jakarta Sans* delivers crisp, geometric legibility across long-form prose and functional UI controls. Its optical clarity grounds the expressive nature of the primary serif.
- **Data & Computations**: *JetBrains Mono* is employed for parametric readouts, numerical axes, raw inputs, and code demonstrations, ensuring fixed-pitch alignment during interactive states.
- **Optical Rules**: Large editorial headlines use negative letter-spacing to preserve optical cohesion, while all uppercase micro-labels utilize positive tracking (+0.04em to +0.06em) for crisp legibility at small dimensions.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to an asymmetrical, editorial broadsheet framework designed for both sustained reading and active simulation.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: Max width `1440px`. The primary layout often leverages an 8/4 split: 8 columns dedicated to the deep explanatory narrative and responsive canvas simulations, alongside 4 sticky columns for interactive parameter decks, formula annotations, and secondary perspectives.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Fluid guttering of `1.5rem` with dynamic vertical reflow where interactive widgets dock beneath their contextual explanations.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Single-column narrative flow with an edge margin of `1.25rem`. Interactive sandboxes switch to full-width card slides with sticky drawer parameter toggles.

### Vertical Rhythm
Long-form editorial sections breathe with deliberate open space (`space-2xl`), demarcated by delicate rules or thematic glyphs, preventing visual clutter when combining dense interactive simulations with long-form prose.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is produced primarily through tonal paper layering, refined ghost outlines, and ultra-soft optical shadows, rejecting generic floating effects in favor of archival precision.

- **The Ground (Base)**: The base parchment field (`#F9F8F5`) is matte and non-reflective.
- **Level 1 (Interactive Plates & Cards)**: Elevated using an ultra-fine structural border (`1px solid #E4E1D9`) and a faint slate-tinted ambient veil: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 34, 40, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Tooltips & Inspector Floats)**: High-translucency surfaces using backdrop blur (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), bounded by `1px solid rgba(228, 225, 217, 0.8)` and a soft dispersion shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 34, 40, 0.08)`.
- **Recessed / Workbench Wells**: Inset inputs and diagram boards use an internal shadow inset (`inset 0 1px 3px rgba(27, 34, 40, 0.05)`) against `#F0EFEA` to establish a physical computational work area.

## Shapes
The design adopts a tailored, intellectual geometric discipline using soft curvature (`roundedness: 1`):
- **Cards, Interactive Windows & Dialogs**: `0.5rem` (`8px`), balancing modern software design with the sharp edges of precision instruments.
- **Interactive Tags, Chips & Control Pills**: Fully rounded pill profiles (`9999px`) to create tangible, touch-friendly affordances against structured rectangular containers.
- **Inputs & Micro-Badges**: `0.25rem` (`4px`) to preserve a crisp, technical look for numerical readouts and code containers.

## Components

### Interactive Canvas & Simulation Cards
- **Structure**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E4E1D9`, corner radius `8px`.
- **Header**: Includes an editorial card title in *Newsreader*, accompanied by domain-coded discipline pills and parameter reset controls.
- **Interactive Viewport**: Inset background (`#F0EFEA`) housing SVG/Canvas-based visual simulations with micro-grid overlays (`rgba(27, 34, 40, 0.04)`).

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Obsidian slate fill (`#1B2228`), text `#FFFFFF`, subtle inner highlight on top edge (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`). Hover transition shifts slightly toward the active domain color.
- **Secondary Action**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E4E1D9`, text `#1B2228`. Hover: `#F0EFEA`.
- **Ghost Action**: Transparent background, text `#4A5560`. Hover: text `#1B2228` with `#E4E1D9` tint.

### Domain Badges & Interactive Pill Tags
- **Structure**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `2px 10px`, typography `label-sm`.
- **States**: 
  - Physics/Math: Background `rgba(0, 164, 180, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 164, 180, 0.25)`, text `#0D828A`.
  - History: Background `rgba(189, 83, 56, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(189, 83, 56, 0.25)`, text `#BD5338`.
  - Natural Sciences: Background `rgba(31, 138, 101, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(31, 138, 101, 0.25)`, text `#1F8A65`.
  - Systems: Background `rgba(91, 72, 217, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(91, 72, 217, 0.25)`, text `#5B48D9`.

### Parameter Sliders & Tactile Controls
- **Track**: `4px` height, `#E4E1D9` background with active fill taking on the domain accent color.
- **Thumb**: Circular `18px`, pure white with `1px solid #E4E1D9`, elevated with `0 2px 6px rgba(27, 34, 40, 0.15)`. Active/Drag: expands slightly to `20px` with an accent-toned focus aura.

### Inputs & Micro-Data Badges
- **Text & Numerical Fields**: Recessed background (`#F0EFEA`), font *JetBrains Mono*, border `1px solid #E4E1D9`. Focus state applies a crisp `1px` outline tinted with the respective domain color.
- **Value Readouts**: Fixed-width mono display badges displaying real-time calculations alongside step increments (`+`, `-`).

### List & Step-Through Sequences
- **Historical Timelines / Proof Derivations**: Left-bordered rail (`1px solid #E4E1D9`) punctuated by numerical index nodes. Active steps light up with deep ink typography and glowing domain accents, while unreached steps remain muted slate (`#7C8793`).
